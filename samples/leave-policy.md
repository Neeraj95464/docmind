# Example Corp Leave Policy

*Fictional sample document for the DocMind demo. Version 2.1, effective 1 April.*

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Example Corp. Contractors and interns follow the terms in their individual agreements.

## 2. Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2 days per month.

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March, after which they lapse. Annual leave cannot be exchanged for cash except on resignation, when unused accrued days are paid out with the final salary.

Annual leave requests of up to 3 days need at least one week of notice. Requests longer than 3 days need at least three weeks of notice and must be approved by the employee's line manager in the HR portal.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry forward. For an absence longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 5 working days of returning to work.

Employees should inform their line manager before 10:00 on the first day of illness.

## 4. Parental leave

Birth parents are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Partners are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 6 months after the birth or adoption. Adoptive parents receive the same entitlement as birth parents.

## 5. Bereavement leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 days for other relatives.

## 6. Public holidays

The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The list is published in the HR portal every December. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, to be taken within 60 days.

## 7. Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

After 3 years of continuous service, employees may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of up to 3 months. Sabbatical requests need at least 2 months of notice and approval from both the line manager and the HR team.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy should be raised with the People Operations team through the HR portal.
